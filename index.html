What's New in v3.0

The Class System & Subclass Specialization

⚔️ Choose Your Class

  • • At Level 8 you now choose Scholar, Warrior, Wizard or Ranger — each with its own 5-node skill tree and a choice of two build-defining capstones
  • • Every perk is free — earned through play, never paywalled — and they hook into everything: focus XP, boss damage, crit chance, spell duration, crystal yield, companion XP and loot luck

🌟 Subclass Specialization

  • • Master your class and reach Level 40 to unlock one of two subclasses — a deeper tree that stacks on top of your existing perks rather than replacing them

💪 Effort Now Pays

  • • High-priority quests now grant 1.5× XP and low-priority ones 0.75× — medium stays exactly as it was, so the demanding quests finally reward more
